## Direction contract

SCOPE: single-page Persuade surface, index.html (with fast ~480ms daylight-default Liquid Glass loader in assets/chv-loader/chv-loader.js and sanitized Gananova OS dashboard preview in assets/gananova-dashboard.svg) — Jose Pablo Chaves Hidalgo's custom software & web development site in Costa Rica, desktop and mobile-first responsive.

THESIS: Custom software and websites built around how each business actually operates — spoken directly in first person without hollow buzzwords or SaaS product sales — anchored in a daylight-first Apple Liquid Glass architectural studio. Refuses red/pink glow on the hero logo slab, redundant header logo in the hero, real client data in dashboard screenshots, pricing/plan/demo buttons on Gananova OS, cryptic telemetry (0.4s, LOAD, 100%, CORE, 60Hz), hollow buzzwords (innovador, revolucionario, soluciones integrales), nested cards, 3D rotating cubes, typewriter effects, and 3D card tilt.

OWN-WORLD: Daylight-default (`data-theme="light"`) Architectural Apple Liquid Glass (`#F2F3F7` stone-pearl ground with frosted pearl glass slabs and crisp `1px` white specular rims; full Dark obsidian `#07070A` parity via toggle). The monumental hero `CHV SYSTEMS` glass slab and crescent orbs stay pure neutral frosted crystal/obsidian with zero red sheen, while lower sections and primary conversion pills carry disciplined crimson-ruby (`#BE123C` / `#E11D48`) accents. Set in Onest for geometric display clarity and Figtree for direct first-person prose.

STORY: Visitor lands in Light mode on the pure neutral frosted `CHV SYSTEMS` Liquid Glass slab (with the top-left header logo hidden until scrolling past the hero), reads Jose Pablo's direct custom-software pitch, explores `#proyectos` structured around Gananova OS as the flagship case study (`Next.js`, `TypeScript`, `PostgreSQL`, `Prisma`, `Docker`), three real industry experience lenses (`Maquinaria pesada`, `Escuelas deportivas`, `Comercio y outlets`), the secondary `Sitio web de Gananova` plus `ADEFIP` and `Pura Vida Painting LLC`, the `"También desarrollo"` bar, and the `"Conversemos"` closing callout — followed by tailored services, an interactive Excel/WhatsApp-vs-custom-system comparison, a 4-step workflow ribbon, Jose Pablo's founder sheet, and a 1-click WhatsApp/email contact selector.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top-right frosted Liquid Glass navigation capsule with plain links, ES/EN & Light/Dark toggles, and `Conversemos ↗` pill (`brand-link` on the top-left stays hidden in `#inicio` and glides in once scrolled past `#heroGlassSlab`). Center stage holds the pure neutral frosted Liquid Glass `CHV SYSTEMS` slab with white specular sweep, 4-point neutral optical star glint, and floating crescent orbs over architectural concrete monoliths, framed by `"Diseño. Desarrollo. A medida."`, first-person custom software pitch + `"Ver proyectos ↗"` / WhatsApp pills, capabilities summary mid-right, and Pérez Zeledón, Costa Rica footer markers.

FORM: Daylight-first Architectural Apple Liquid Glass with GPU-only (`transform`, `opacity`) motion choreography: staged hero entrance (`heroGlideUp`), orbital glass-sphere float (`orbFloatTL` / `orbFloatBR`), autonomous pure-white diagonal specular sweep (`slabSpecularSweep`), subtle scroll depth parallax, pointer-tracked caustic light, and staggered scroll-reveal cascades (`.stagger-item`).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
