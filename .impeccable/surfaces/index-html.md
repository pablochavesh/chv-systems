## Direction contract

SCOPE: single-page Persuade surface, index.html — CHV Systems marketing/lead-gen site.

THESIS: This is proof that one person architects and ships the operating system a business actually runs on — not a portfolio of pretty pages. Refuses the generic freelancer template: no icon-plus-heading-plus-text card grid as page structure, no kicker/eyebrow labels, no fake hero-metric stat rows, no section numbers.

OWN-WORLD: User-pinned, binding. Deep navy-to-near-black void ground (#050B18 → #0B1830 range) as the dominant field; one glowing accent hue (electric cyan/azure) carries emphasis and interactive states, a warm gold reserved only for primary CTAs. A thin glowing constellation/circuit linework motif (nodes + pulse lines) is the recurring graphic device, standing in for "connected business systems" (web · CRM · facturación · KPIs · automatización) — never decorative blur/glass for its own sake. Hairline 1px panel borders, no nested cards. Display type is a confident geometric/grotesk sans with a point of view (Space Grotesk or Instrument Sans family) for headlines; a monospace face is reserved for real data labels (module names, status lines) only, never as a generic "tech" costume. Light mode swaps the ground to a crisp cool-white/pale-blue field and renders the same constellation motif in ink-navy + the same accent hue — same identity, not an inverted palette.

STORY: Visitor lands and immediately reads "this person builds real business systems" from the live constellation graphic + a direct headline (not a mood). Scrolls through: services (design / development / web systems), a live-system proof panel built from Gananova's real feature set, a process section, a founder section (Jose Pablo Chaves Hidalgo, Systems Engineering student, framed as current/hands-on credibility, not a liability), a work showcase with three real projects (Gananova — live, ADEFIP — in progress, Pura Vida Painting — delivered), honestly labeled, and a closing CTA to start a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark navy hero. Headline occupies the left ~60% at display scale, tight tracking, stating what CHV Systems builds. A live animated constellation graph (SVG/canvas) sits behind/right — labeled nodes (WEB, CRM, FACTURACIÓN, KPIs, AUTOMATIZACIÓN) joined by glowing pulse lines, plus a slow aurora-beam sweep behind the whole hero. Primary CTA ("Iniciar un proyecto") and a secondary link to the Gananova proof sit under the headline. A single honest status line replaces a fake stat block, e.g. "Sistema en producción — Gananova, en uso por negocios reales."

FORM: Brief-pinned world (dark navy / glow / dark-default with light-mode toggle) supplied directly by the user; no concept-seed roll run — a pinned constraint is not re-rolled. Composition/structure above chosen directly against the Persuade mode and the audience (SME owners and international small-business clients deciding whether to trust one person with a real piece of their business).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
