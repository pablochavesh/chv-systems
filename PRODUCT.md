# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single-file-friendly (also published as a Claude Artifact for preview/sharing), deployable as-is to Hostinger. Decided by delivery context (a personal-brand marketing site, no backend needed) and confirmed by the user ("ambos": Artifact preview + real files in this folder).

## Users

Small and medium business owners and entrepreneurs (primarily Costa Rica/LatAm) who need a professional web presence or a working business system (invoicing, CRM, inventory, reporting), plus a smaller segment of international clients (e.g. a US-based small business owner). They are evaluating whether to trust an independent developer/studio with a real, revenue-generating piece of their business — not just a brochure site.

## Product Purpose

CHV Systems is the personal studio/brand of Jose Pablo Chaves Hidalgo, a Systems Engineering student in Costa Rica, who designs and builds websites and web systems (business management platforms, invoicing, dashboards) for clients. This site exists to convert visitors into inquiries/leads by demonstrating real, in-production work rather than claims.

## Positioning

Not a template shop or a generic freelance portfolio: CHV Systems ships full working *systems* (e.g. a live multi-client business-management + invoicing + KPI platform), not just marketing pages — the same person designs the interface and builds the backend logic behind it.

## Operating Context

Real evidence available:
- **Gananova** (www.gananova.com, live) — the Costa Rican strategy/process consultancy for SMEs where Jose Pablo currently works, tagline "Estrategia · Procesos · Tecnología." He built **Gananova OS**, an advanced business management system with AI and automation (CRM, invoicing, real-time KPIs/metrics) used there. Present this as his workplace + a system he built there, not as a client project he was contracted for.

- **Pura Vida Painting** (www.puravidapaintingllc.com) — a painting & pressure-washing business; a real client based in the United States. Project delivered.

No other case studies, testimonials, pricing, or client logos exist yet; do not fabricate quotes, client counts, or metrics beyond what's stated above.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend/CMS on this marketing site itself — it is a static, self-contained page.
- Contact mechanism: a contact section/CTA (form can be a `mailto:`/static form; no server to process submissions in this deliverable).
- Bilingual note: primary language is Spanish (the user writes in Spanish); copy should be written in Spanish.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **CHV SYSTEMS**. Tagline given by the user: "WEB DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT BY CHV SYSTEMS."
- Founder: **Jose Pablo Chaves Hidalgo**, Systems Engineering student, Costa Rica — to be credited in an About/Founder section.
- Visual constraint pinned directly by the user (binding, not to be re-rolled): dark theme by default, navy-blue-led palette, glowing/light-flare background accents, with a light-mode toggle that preserves the same premium "essence" (not a flattened generic light theme).

## Evidence on Hand

Real: Gananova (live product + real feature set, sourced from www.gananova.com), ADEFIP (in-progress, no live link to show), Pura Vida Painting (delivered, no live link/screenshot on hand). No screenshots or photography were supplied for any of the three; portfolio cards will use crafted graphic/UI representations (not fabricated screenshots) and label ADEFIP's status honestly as in progress.

## Product Principles

1. Prove capability with real, working systems (Gananova) rather than generic "I build websites" claims.
2. Respect the pinned dark-navy/light-flare identity at every surface; the light mode is a variant of the same world, not a different brand.
3. Write for SME owners deciding whether to trust one person with a real piece of their business — clarity and confidence over cleverness.
4. Never invent client counts, revenue numbers, testimonials, or pricing not confirmed by the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Both theme modes must meet WCAG contrast minimums (body/placeholder text ≥4.5:1); the light mode must not be an afterthought.
