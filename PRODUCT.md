## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single-file-friendly (also published as a Claude Artifact for preview/sharing), deployable as-is to Hostinger. Decided by delivery context (a personal-brand marketing site, no backend needed) and confirmed by the user ("ambos": Artifact preview + real files in this folder).

## Users

Small and medium business owners, shops, restaurants, workshops, sports academies, and entrepreneurs (primarily Costa Rica/LatAm, plus US small-business clients) browsing predominantly on mobile phones as well as laptops. They want a modern website that attracts customers or a simple custom program to organize sales, quotes, payments, and daily work — explained in clear, everyday words without technical jargon.

## Product Purpose

CHV Systems is the personal studio/brand of Jose Pablo Chaves Hidalgo, a Systems Engineering student in Costa Rica, who designs and builds websites and easy-to-use business programs for clients. This site exists to convert visitors into inquiries/leads by showing real finished projects and explaining everything simply.

## Positioning

Not a template shop or a confusing agency: CHV Systems creates both clean, high-trust websites that bring customers straight to WhatsApp and complete custom business tools (like Gananova OS) — with 1-on-1 direct communication with the person who designs and builds everything.

## Product Principles

1. Explain every service in warm, simple, everyday words any business owner understands immediately — zero technical jargon.
2. Commit fully to the sculptural Architectural Liquid Glass + subtle crimson-ruby material system from the user's reference, adapting effortlessly to mobile screens.
3. Prove capability with real finished projects in Costa Rica and the United States (`Gananova`, `ADEFIP`, `Pura Vida Painting LLC`, `Gananova OS`).
4. Never invent client counts, revenue numbers, testimonials, or pricing not confirmed by the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Both Dark and Light Liquid Glass modes must maintain WCAG AA contrast minimums (body/placeholder text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1) over refractive surfaces, provide comfortable ≥44px mobile touch targets, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
