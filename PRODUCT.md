## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single-file-friendly (also published as a Claude Artifact for preview/sharing), deployable as-is to Hostinger. Decided by delivery context (a personal-brand marketing site, no backend needed) and confirmed by the user ("ambos": Artifact preview + real files in this folder).

## Users

Business owners across heavy machinery/fleet operations, sports academies, retail stores and outlets, SMEs, and service companies (primarily in Costa Rica, plus US small-business clients) browsing on mobile phones and laptops. They want custom software tailored to how their operation actually works — or a clean, credible website — explained directly in first person without hollow buzzwords or technical jargon.

## Product Purpose

CHV Systems is the personal brand of Jose Pablo Chaves Hidalgo, a custom software developer and Systems Engineering student in Costa Rica. This site exists to showcase real custom software experience (led by Gananova OS as his primary case study) and real published websites, converting business owners who still rely on spreadsheets and WhatsApp threads into direct 1-on-1 conversations.

## Positioning

Custom software developer in Costa Rica (`"Software hecho a la medida de cada operación"`): systems and websites born from how each business actually works, not the other way around. **Must NOT look like selling a SaaS product**: **Gananova OS** is Jose Pablo's primary engineering case study — presented with **no prices, no plans, and no "comprar" or "probar demo" buttons**. Direct, first-person voice (`contame`, `trabajás`), zero empty buzzwords (`"innovador"`, `"revolucionario"`, `"soluciones integrales"`).

No other case studies, testimonials, pricing, client names inside private dashboards, or metrics exist; never fabricate clients, figures, testimonials, or unconfirmed features. Real client data in dashboard previews must be replaced with clearly labeled fictitious sample data.

## Product Principles

1. Speak directly in first person about real operational software and websites — zero SaaS pricing/demo gimmicks and zero hollow buzzwords.
2. Default to crisp daylight Apple Liquid Glass (`data-theme="light"`), keeping the hero `CHV SYSTEMS` slab pure neutral crystal and hiding the redundant header logo until scrolling past the hero.
3. Prove capability through Gananova OS, three concrete industry workflows, and real live websites (`Gananova`, `ADEFIP`, `Pura Vida Painting LLC`) while keeping private client dashboard data strictly fictitious.
4. Use smooth GPU-only (`transform`, `opacity`) Liquid Glass motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Both Light (default) and Dark Liquid Glass modes must maintain WCAG AA contrast minimums (body/placeholder text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1) over refractive surfaces, provide comfortable ≥44px mobile touch targets, and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
